ClipForge

A personal automation tool that turns clips from live streams into vertical short-form videos and publishes them to TikTok on behalf of the account owner who operates it.

What it does

  • Discovers public Twitch clips through the official Twitch API.
  • Applies compliance filters: language, duration, popularity, an allow-list of authorized source channels, and detection of segments muted for copyright claims.
  • Transcribes the audio locally and generates captions.
  • Reframes the video to vertical format and burns in the captions.
  • Publishes the result to TikTok, on the operator's own account, always crediting the original creator of the clip.

Runs entirely on the operator's own computer. There is no server, no hosted service and no remote database. All video processing, transcription and credential storage happens locally.

Who uses it

The tool is operated by a single person, on their own TikTok account. It is not offered as a service to third parties and it does not process any other person's data.

Attribution

Every published video identifies the source channel of the clip, both on screen and in the post description. Only channels that expressly authorize the use of their clips are included in the allow-list of sources.
